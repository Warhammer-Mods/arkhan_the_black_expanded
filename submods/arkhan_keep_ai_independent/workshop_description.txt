Keep AI-controlled Arkhan the Black independent from Nagash.

This optional companion to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2852724610]Arkhan the Black: Expanded[/url] blocks Nagash from acquiring AI Arkhan through diplomatic confederation, Mortarch subjugation or the Nagash endgame acquisition path.

Player-controlled Arkhan is unaffected. The restriction applies whether Nagash is controlled by a player or the AI. Other Mortarchs are unchanged.

[h1]Installation[/h1]
Enable this pack alongside the main mod. The pack itself is the option: there is no in-game checkbox. Disable or remove the earlier TEST version before enabling this release.

Works by applying protection when the campaign loads and refreshing it on Arkhan’s and Nagash’s faction turns. It does not restore Arkhan if he has already been confederated.

[h1]Notes[/h1]
The tested script is unchanged for release. This is a script-only submod, with no vanilla script replacements or DB changes. In-game testing has been confirmed; multiplayer has not been verified.

Removing the pack stops its protection script. Restrictions already stored in a campaign save may remain; use a save from before enabling it if you want to restore the previous campaign state.
