# Black Arts — optional Arkhan research add-on (test build)

Requires Arkhan the Black: Expanded with its separate Necromantic Energy network.
Enable this pack alongside the main mod. The main pack works without it.

Adds an Arkhan-only third technology tab, Black Arts: three four-stage paths
for servants, spirits and monsters, followed by The Great Awakening.
Research costs 300, 600, 1,000 and 1,500 energy along each path; the final
research costs 2,000. Total permanent research cost: 12,200 energy.
The costs use native technology resource transactions linked exclusively to
Arkhan's separate resource. Existing technologies and income are unchanged.

The final research unlocks an optional offer every five turns when at least
750 energy is available. Accept to spend 750 for five turns of +4%
replenishment, +10 Winds of Magic capacity and +10% spell mastery; decline
to retain the reserve. Offers and pending choices persist in saves. The rite
uses the real Arkhan regional-network resource and rechecks affordability.

Tomb Kings: Extended compatibility is UNVERIFIED. Separate keys and files
avoid overwriting its records, but both mods can affect the same technology
node set and tab layout. Do not advertise compatibility until tested.

Test: load a copied Arkhan save, check all three tabs at your UI scale,
verify icons, localisation and prerequisites. Check native research charges
against Arkhan's network and that Nagash's reserve stays unchanged; test
insufficient funds and switching research. Complete the final technology,
accept/decline the rite, save/reload a pending offer, and advance five turns.
Repeat with TKE and then with matching multiplayer packs/load orders.
If research cannot resolve the regional resource, stop the test and report
that behaviour; no alternate/shared pool is used as a fallback.

Disable the add-on to roll back; keep your pre-test save.
