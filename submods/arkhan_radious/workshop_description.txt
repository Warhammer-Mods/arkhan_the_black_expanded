[h1]Arkhan the Black for Radious[/h1]

This is the Radious submod for Arkhan the Black: Expanded. It includes the existing Radious compatibility changes, updated for the current Arkhan roster, and a full set of sandy unit cards to match the other Tomb Kings units in Radious.

[h1]What's included[/h1]

[list]
[*]Sandy artwork for Arkhan's units, Regiments of Renown, Lords and Heroes.
[*]Small army cards and larger information cards, with soft edges on the larger artwork.
[*]Updated character cards so the different Lord and Hero appearances share the same style.
[*]The existing Radious unit and building changes, with balance carried across to the renamed Necrarch Lords, Liche High Priests and Cairn Wraiths.
[/list]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2852724610]Arkhan the Black: Expanded[/url]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2791750313]Radious Total War Mod - Part 1[/url]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2791750075]Radious Total War Mod - Part 3[/url]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2813892035]Radious Total War Mod - Part 4 - Assets[/url]

Enable all of the required mods and this submod. Give this submod higher priority than Arkhan the Black: Expanded and Radious. If you have a separate older copy of the Arkhan Radious patch, disable it to avoid loading both versions.

[h1]Balance and compatibility[/h1]

The existing Radious stat values have been kept. Newer units without a counterpart in the older Radious patch still use their main-mod values and may need further balancing.

Arkhan's campaign features come from the main mod. This submod requires both Arkhan the Black: Expanded and Radious.

Please report bugs [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2865162727]on this Workshop page[/url]. Include your active mods, whether you started a new campaign or loaded a save, and whether the issue occurs in single-player or multiplayer.
