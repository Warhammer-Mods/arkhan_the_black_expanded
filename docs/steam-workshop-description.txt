Version: 4.0.1

This is the vanilla version of Arkhan the Black: Expanded for Total War: Warhammer III.

This mod adds new technologies, skills and abilities to Lords and Heroes. Arkhan also has his own variants of the vanilla Tomb Kings units, with unique unit cards and a mirrored Realm of Souls ability with a different visual effect. New buildings allow recruitment of additional units not found among the other Tomb Kings factions.

[h1]Land of the Dead and latest additions[/h1]

[list]
[*]Establish Necropolises and devastate eligible provinces through the Ritual of Undeath. Wizard Caliph's Palace is Arkhan's campaign anchor, replacing Nagash's Black Pyramid.
[*]Arkhan has separate Necromantic Energy, independent of Nagash. Supported buildings and post-battle sources provide it; the devastation ritual spends 500 energy from Arkhan's pool. Necropolises grant access in their province and adjacent provinces.
[*]Energy, Necropolis count and soft cap appear in the top bar, with energy and Arkhan tooltips in the province UI. The map overlay is hidden, as for Nagash.
[*]The soft cap starts at one, grows by one per three devastated provinces, and includes limit bonuses. Each excess Necropolis reduces energy income by 10%, up to 100%.
[*]Necropolis data and counters refresh on save load, construction, demolition and faction turns. Migration separates the old campaign-group link without changing Nagash's reserve.
[*]New campaigns start with the custom Tomb Herald replacing the original hero and requesting level one. Existing campaigns skip this replacement.
[*]Updated Arkhan-themed cards, a purple technology tab and dedicated Arkhan/Wizard Caliph's Palace localisation retain original icon sizes, soft edges, localisation calls and tooltip line breaks.
[*]Current unit, building, skill and asset updates are merged with these mechanics. Superseded fragments are removed; building conditions recognise Arkhan's devastation. Source exports preserve native data where needed; new DB fragment names do not end in numbers.
[*]GitHub workflows use current actions, validate DB/localisation and Lua, and build downloadable packs from game assets. Lua warnings are nonblocking; errors fail.
[/list]
